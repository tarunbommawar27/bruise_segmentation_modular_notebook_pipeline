  {\LARGE\bfseries Bruise Segmentation Results Summary}\\[4pt]
  {\large Saved-Model Evaluation: Native-Argmax YOLO and Subject-Level Analysis}
\end{center}

\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{Models.} The five \emph{saved} project checkpoints (loaded and scored, no
retraining): a SegFormer-B2 teacher, SegFormer-B0 direct and distilled students, and
YOLO26n-sem direct and distilled.

\textbf{Evaluation set.} 185 white-light images from 28 subjects, scored against the
2-of-3 majority-vote mask on a common $640\times640$ grid. SegFormer thresholds fitted on
the 134-image validation set (1-SE tie-band sweep); YOLO uses native argmax.

\textbf{YOLO evaluation.} Native argmax only. The custom /255 YOLO path is excluded.

\textbf{Source.} Consolidated from \texttt{docs/saved\_models\_report.pdf}. Section~4
per-group figures are recomputed from the saved-model per-image outputs
(SegFormer \texttt{fixed\_test\_evaluation}, YOLO native), so small numeric differences
from the report's figure-based values (a $<0.01$ Dice threshold refinement) are expected.

\textbf{Main observation.}
On these saved checkpoints the three SegFormer models cluster tightly (0.773--0.786 mean
Dice), and the \emph{distilled} B0 (0.786) is the top mean scorer---edging the B2 teacher
(0.773). This ``student beats teacher'' ordering is specific to the saved checkpoints; it
did not survive the batch-equalised retraining. The clearest practical separation is the
complete-miss rate: SegFormer-B2 and distilled B0 are essentially miss-free, whereas
native-argmax YOLO blanks 7--10\% of images despite a comparable (higher) median.

\textbf{Interpretation.}
At 28 subjects, distillation and student-vs-teacher are both unresolvable Dice contrasts.
Notably, on these saved checkpoints \emph{even the SegFormer$-$YOLO Dice contrast is not
significant} ($+0.051$, CI spans zero)---unlike the batch-equalised retrained run. The one
clearly resolved result is the higher complete-miss rate of YOLO-direct relative to
SegFormer-B0 distilled.

subject-clustered regression \texttt{recall $\sim$ log$_{10}$(size) + light} isolates the
light-skin term at equal size (from the saved report, Table~3; negative = worse on light

\section{Final Takeaway}

On the saved checkpoints, SegFormer-B0 distilled matches the B2 teacher's Dice (it is
nominally the top scorer) at about twice the throughput and 7.4$\times$ fewer parameters.
The paired analysis does \emph{not} establish a Dice gain from distillation, nor even a
significant SegFormer$-$YOLO Dice gap at this sample size; distillation's practical value
here is compact deployment with a near-zero complete-miss rate. Native-argmax YOLO is
substantially faster but its higher complete-miss rate---concentrated on the smallest,
lightest-skin lesions---remains the principal limitation. Every model beats the
three-expert human--human agreement ceiling (0.639 mean Dice), so model-to-model Dice
differences here sit below the annotation-noise floor.
